Pinned folders

OpenClose

Open a folder and tap the pin. It stays on Home, one tap away, however deep it really lives.

  • Up to 8 pins on Home
  • Unlimited saved folders, with colours and groups
  • Shortcuts on your phone's home screen
  • Saved folders follow when you rename or move them in Qabinet

Locked and hidden folders

OpenClose

Locked folders are encrypted, so other apps and a connected computer can't read them. Hidden folders simply stay out of sight.

  • Locked: AES-256 encryption of files and file names
  • Locks again when you leave the app
  • A recovery key if you forget your password
  • Hidden: kept out of lists, search and gallery apps, but not encrypted

Copying and moving

OpenClose

Every copy is read back and compared with the original before Qabinet removes anything.

  • Copy, browse anywhere, then paste
  • Pause or cancel long transfers
  • Compare both files when names clash
  • A Recycle bin that keeps deleted items for 30 days

Finding and tidying

OpenClose

Search your whole phone by name, type, size or date, or start from a category on Home.

  • List and grid views, sorted the way you like
  • Rename many files at once
  • Create and open ZIP files, with passwords
  • Light, dark and wallpaper colours

Your phone, nothing else

OpenClose

This version has no internet permission, so Qabinet can't send your files anywhere.

  • Internal storage, SD cards and USB drives
  • No ads and no trackers
  • Network drives and cloud storage are planned for version 1.0

Install the beta

  1. Download the file on your Android phone. It needs Android 8 or newer.
  2. Open it from your downloads. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Open Qabinet and allow file access so it can show your folders.
